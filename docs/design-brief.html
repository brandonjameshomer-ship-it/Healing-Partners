What I need to design real memorials.

Right now the designer draws six invented shapes in six invented stones, with dimensions I made up. It looks convincing, which is exactly the danger — a funeral director would spot it as fiction in about four seconds.

Below is what turns it into something you could put in front of a family. It's ordered so the first section unblocks the most, and you can stop at any point and I'll build with what I have.

You don't have to write any of this out. Photographs of your samples, a vendor catalogue PDF, or your current price list will teach me more than typed answers — and I can read all three. Talking it through works too.
1

What you actually sell

This is the one that matters most. Every shape currently in the app is a guess.

Which forms do you offer?Start here

The industry has standard categories. Tell me which of these you sell, and which you don't:

  • Upright monuments (die and base)
  • Slant markers
  • Bevel markers
  • Flat / grass markers
  • Ledgers
  • Benches
  • Columbarium and niche plaques
  • Companion versions of any of the above
And the one that matters commercially: which is your best seller?

What sizes do they come in?

Monuments are sold in fixed sizes, not arbitrary ones. I need the real list per form — width × height × thickness — including which is standard and which costs extra.

For example, if a flat marker is 24″ × 12″ × 4″ and that's the standard, say so — I'll make it the default.

What shape variations exist within a form?

An upright can have a serpentine top, an oval top, a half-serp, a straight top, shoulders. Which tops do you actually cut, and are some priced differently?

2

Stone

The six colours in the app are approximations I mixed by eye. Real granite has trade names, and directors and families know them.

Which granites do you stock or order?Start here

Trade names, please — they're what customers see in catalogues.

Barre Gray, Georgia Gray, Absolute / India Black, Dakota Mahogany, Missouri Red, Bahama Blue, Blue Pearl, and so on.

Can you photograph a sample of each?

A phone photo of each sample in daylight is genuinely the best thing you could send me. I'll pull the real colour and grain out of it so the preview matches the stone the family will actually get.

Which finishes do you offer, and where?

Polished, steeled, sawn, rock pitch, frosted — and typically which faces get which. A polished front with rock-pitch sides looks completely different from an all-polished die, and I should draw that difference.

Does stone colour change the price, or just the look?

If black granite carries a premium over grey, I need the relationship, not the absolute numbers.

3

Lettering and artwork

The app currently offers "Traditional / Modern / Script", which is not how this industry names anything.

Which alphabets do you cut?

Roman, Modified Roman, Old English, Gothic, script faces — whatever your shop or vendor actually offers, by their real names.

How is the lettering cut?

V-sunk, flat-sunk, raised, or laser-etched — they catch light completely differently, and right now I'm drawing one generic incision for everything.

What's in your emblem catalogue?

I invented nine symbols. What do you really offer — and is there a vendor emblem book I can work from?

Do you do etching or portraits?

Laser etching of photographs, scenes, or portraits is a significant upsell for a lot of shops. If you offer it, it should be in the designer.

How it's priced

Your deck says designs update in real time by style, size and cost. Cost is the piece I have nothing for — and it's the feature that closes sales.

What drives the base price?Start here

Typically form + size + stone. If you can give me even a rough grid, the designer can show a live figure as the family builds.

What's included, and what's extra?

  • Is a set number of letters included, or is it charged per character or per line?
  • Emblems — included, flat fee, or per design?
  • Etching and portraits
  • Foundation and setting
  • Delivery, and does distance matter?
  • A second date added later, when the surviving spouse dies

Should families see prices, or only funeral homes?

This is a real decision and it changes the product. Some shops show families a figure immediately; others quote only through the director. I can build either, or show families a range and directors the exact number.

How does the 15 / 12 / 10% share get counted?

Is the share on the marker sale price, or on your wholesale figure? And to bill it, the system has to count memorials — which is the first thing that genuinely needs a server rather than a browser.

5

The rules that constrain a design

A designer that lets a family fall in love with something the cemetery won't permit is worse than no designer at all.

What do cemeteries restrict?

Flat-only sections, maximum heights, thickness minimums, approved materials, single versus companion footprints. If the app knows the cemetery, it can stop a family designing something that will be refused.

Who checks that today?

The director, the monument dealer, or the family? Whoever it is, that step could move into the software.

What's the approval process?

Most shops send a proof for signature before cutting. If Remember Them produced that proof, it would slot straight into how the trade already works.
