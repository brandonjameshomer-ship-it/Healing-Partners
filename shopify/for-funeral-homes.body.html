How it works

It asks about the person, not the product.

A thick catalogue is not a service. It is the reason families cannot decide. So the software does the narrowing, and the family only ever answers questions about someone they loved.

  1. Their name and their years

    One screen, one thing on it. Nothing else is asked yet.

  2. An interview, not a form

    Stories by life stage — childhood, work, marriage, the years of raising people. Then habits, hobbies, talents, and the ten words the family would use to describe them. It follows up the way a good biographer would.

  3. The family says what matters most

    They rate each answer from one to ten for how much it should shape the memorial. That is the narrowing, and it takes a minute.

  4. Three memorials come back

    Real granite, real dimensions, priced from your supplier's own sheet — then the order form, filled out. If none of the three fit, it goes again.

The question every director asks

Short answer: it stays yours.

  • Nothing is used to train AI. Not what the family writes, not your business data, not ever — unless you separately agree to it in writing. That is a term of the partner agreement, not a policy that can be quietly revised.
  • You are the controller, we are the processor. The family's information is yours in the legal sense as well as the practical one.
  • Each funeral home sees only its own families. The home down the road cannot see your orders, your prices, or what you are billed. That separation is enforced in the database, not on the screens, so it holds even if someone goes looking.
  • You can take it with you. Export any time during your term and for thirty days after it ends. Deletion follows within ninety days of termination.
  • Families see their own memorial and nothing about how your business runs.

Before you start

Straight answers.

Three days isn't long. What can I actually do in it?

Enough, if you don't wait for a family. Recreate the last three memorials you sold — you already know what those families chose, so you can judge the result honestly — then design one for someone you loved. About half an hour, and it answers the question better than a walkthrough would.

If you need longer because you are waiting on a particular family, email me and I will extend it. I would rather you tried it properly than decided against it because the calendar was awkward.

Do I have to change suppliers?

No. It works from the price lists you already use and fills in the order forms your monument companies already send you. It sits on top of the relationships you have, and you upload a new price list yourself whenever one changes.

It is built for several manufacturers, not one — granite, bronze, cast glass, inlay, benches, cremation pedestals. Each supplier keeps its own catalogue, its own finishes and its own order form.

Do you make the headstone?

No, and it matters that you know it. Healing Partners is not a monument manufacturer, an installer, or a death‑care provider. We produce the design, the proof and the production file. You choose the fabricator, and the physical transaction stays entirely yours — including the margin on it.

What happens to my family service counselor?

They stay in the room. The software does not sell. It removes the part of their job that was paperwork and revision cycles, and what is left is the part they are actually good at. They get to do more of it, on more families.

Will my staff be able to use it?

A family reaches a finished design in ten clicks or fewer and can always go back and choose differently. If it needed a training day it would have failed. Nothing to install — it runs in the browser you already have, on a desk or a tablet.

How does approval work? I can't have a proof going to the cutter unconfirmed.

Approval is a deliberate, recorded act. An authorised approver works through the checklist — names, dates, inscription text and punctuation, dimensions, materials and finishes, placement of photographs and emblems, cemetery requirements — and confirms it in the platform. Who approved it, when, which version and the file's fingerprint are all recorded.

An email saying "looks good," a text, a phone call, or silence does not count as approval. That is the point of it.

Why a share of the sale on top of a subscription?

It ties what we earn to what you sell. The subscription keeps the software running; the share means we only do well when orders actually go through it. It falls as your volume rises, so the homes using it most pay the least on each one — and less again if you carry professional liability cover.

Can I cancel?

On the month‑to‑month rate, any month, nothing owed. On the discounted partner rate you have committed to six months, and leaving early recaptures the discount you already received — $200 for each month you had it. Cancel during the three free days on either plan and you are not charged at all.
