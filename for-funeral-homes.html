How it works

It asks about the person, not the product.

A thick catalogue isn't a service — it's the reason families can't decide. So the software does the narrowing instead of asking the family to do it.

  1. They tell you about them

    "She fed everyone who walked through the door." That's enough to start.

  2. It asks two more things

    The questions a good interviewer would ask. What did their hands look like?

  3. It narrows the options

    One suggestion at a time. Yes, maybe, or no — with the reason attached.

  4. Three memorials come back

    Real granite, real sizes, priced from your supplier's sheet. Then the order form.

The question everyone asks

Whose information is this, and where does it go?

Short answer: it stays yours.

  • Each funeral home sees only its own families. Your neighbour down the road cannot see your orders, your prices, or what you're billed.
  • Your counselors see the orders they're working on. Nothing else.
  • Families see their own memorial and nothing about your business.
  • That separation is enforced by the database itself, not by the screens — so it holds even if someone goes looking.
  • What a family writes about the person is never sold, never used for advertising, and never sent anywhere raw. Identifying details are stripped before anything leaves.

Before you start

Straight answers.

Three days isn’t long. What can I actually do in it?

Enough, if you don’t wait for a family. Recreate the last three memorials you sold — you already know what those families chose, so you can judge the result honestly — then design one for someone you loved. That is about half an hour, and it answers the question better than a walkthrough would.

If you need longer because you’re waiting on a particular family, email me and I’ll extend it. I would rather you try it properly than decide against it because the calendar was awkward.

Do I have to change suppliers?

No. It works from the price lists you already use and fills in the order forms your monument companies already send you. It sits on top of the relationships you have, and you can upload a new price list yourself whenever one changes.

What happens to my family service counselor?

They stay in the room. The software doesn’t sell — it removes the part of their job that was paperwork and revisions. What’s left is the part they’re actually good at, and they get to do more of it.

Will my staff be able to use it?

A family reaches a finished design in ten clicks or fewer, and can always go back and choose differently. If it needed a training day it would have failed. Nothing to install — it runs in the browser you already have, on a desk or a tablet.

Why a share of the sale on top of the subscription?

It ties what I earn to what you sell. The subscription keeps the software running; the share means I only do well when orders are actually going through it. It falls as your volume rises, so the homes using it most pay the least on each one.

Can I cancel?

On the $350 month-to-month rate, any month, with nothing owed. On the discounted $150 partner rate you have committed to six months, and leaving early recaptures the discount — $200 for each month you had it. Cancel during the three days and you are not charged at all.
