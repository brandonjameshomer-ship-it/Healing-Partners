Questions

Do families really pay nothing?

Nothing to design. You pay for the memorial you order, the way you always would — the designer is just how you decide what you want before ordering it.

What happens after the seven days?

The designer locks, but nothing you made is deleted. Subscribe and every design is exactly where you left it.

Can we cancel?

Any time, and you keep access through the month you've paid for.

Is our families' information stored anywhere?

No. Designs stay in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded, and we hold no records about the people you serve.
