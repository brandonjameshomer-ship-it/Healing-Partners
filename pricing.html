Questions

Do families really pay nothing?

Nothing to design. You pay for the memorial you order, the way you always would — the designer is just how you decide what you want before ordering it.

What happens after the free trial?

The trial runs seven days, or your first seven designs — whichever ends later — and designs made during it do not count against any allotment. After that the designer locks, but nothing you made is deleted. Subscribe and every design is exactly where you left it.

Do you take a share of what we sell?

No. There is no commission, no fee per memorial, no proof fee and no setup fee, and we have no right to look at your books, your invoices or your prices. You pay a subscription for the software and nothing else. That is written into the agreement, and it is deliberate: a vendor who audits funeral homes is not a vendor this trade should have.

Can we cancel?

Any month, on thirty days’ notice, with nothing further owed. There is no minimum term and no early-exit charge. If you change your mind after your first invoice, tell us within seven days and it is refunded in full. You keep access through the month you have paid for, anything you have made stays licensed to you, and you can export everything for sixty days after you leave. We delete it within ninety days.

Is our families' information stored anywhere?

Designs stay in your own browser as you work — nothing is uploaded while you are designing. Where we do hold records so you can come back to them, you are the controller of that information and we only process it for you: you can export it any time during your subscription and for sixty days after, and we delete it within ninety days of you leaving.

Do you train AI on what we put in?

No. Nothing you or a family enters is used to train AI models, and we will not do so without asking you in writing first. It is a term of the agreement, not a policy we can quietly change.
