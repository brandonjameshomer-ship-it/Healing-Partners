Questions

Do families really pay nothing?

Nothing to design. You pay for the memorial you order, the way you always would — the designer is just how you decide what you want before ordering it.

What happens after the three days?

The designer locks, but nothing you made is deleted. Subscribe and every design is exactly where you left it.

Why a revenue share on top of the subscription?

Because it keeps us honest. The subscription is deliberately low so cost isn't a barrier to trying this. The share means we only do well when you sell more markers — so our work goes into helping families decide with confidence, not into selling you features. And it falls as your volume rises: the more you use it, the less each sale costs you.

Can we cancel?

On the $350 month-to-month rate, any month, with nothing further owed. The $150 rate is discounted in exchange for a six-month commitment, so leaving it early recaptures the discount already taken — a flat $200, however many months you had it. Either way you keep access through the month you have paid for, and nothing you have made is deleted.

Is our families' information stored anywhere?

Designs stay in your own browser as you work — nothing is uploaded while you are designing. Where we do hold records so you can come back to them, you are the controller of that information and we only process it for you: you can export it any time during your subscription and for thirty days after, and we delete it within ninety days of you leaving.

Do you train AI on what we put in?

No. Nothing you or a family enters is used to train AI models, and we will not do so without asking you in writing first. It is a term of the agreement, not a policy we can quietly change.
