The problem

Grief, permanence, and too many choices add up to decision paralysis.

Families are asked to make permanent decisions at their worst moment. The options are hard to understand and harder to compare, and a brochure has never helped anyone picture a headstone standing in a cemetery. Funeral homes lose the chance to serve those families well.
