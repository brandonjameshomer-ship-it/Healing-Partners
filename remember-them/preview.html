The inscription, before and after

The old renderer filled the lettering with a colour darker than the stone on every material. On black granite that put a near-black name on a dark grey face. Sandblasting frosts the cut so it reads paler than the polish around it — which is exactly why black granite is the premium choice.

Every shape, every material

Bronze is cast rather than cut, so its letters stand proud and catch light on top instead of sitting in shadow. White marble gets no contrast from frosting, so the cut reads as its own shadow. Both are handled separately from the granites.

A screen cannot reproduce stone, bronze, etching or polish, and the difference between this preview and the finished memorial is not a design error — Partner User Agreement §3.8. Every customer-facing surface that shows a design carries that notice.
