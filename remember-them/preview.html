The inscription, before and after

The old renderer filled the lettering with a colour darker than the stone on every material. On black granite that put a near-black name on a dark grey face. Sandblasting frosts the cut so it reads paler than the polish around it — which is exactly why black granite is the premium choice.

Every shape, every render family

One cell per render family, not per colour. AFM sells four black granites at four prices; on a screen they are one dark polished rock, so the catalogue keeps them apart and the renderer does not pretend to. Bronze is cast rather than cut, so its letters stand proud and catch light on top instead of sitting in shadow. White marble gets no contrast from frosting, so the cut reads as its own shadow.

The catalogue, and what it costs

Everything below comes out of catalogue.js and is priced by Catalogue.price() at run time — so if this section renders, the price engine works. Wholesale is what the funeral home pays the monument company; retail is that × 2.5, which is a suggestion the Partner is free to change. A family never sees the left column.

A screen cannot reproduce stone, bronze, etching or polish, and the difference between this preview and the finished memorial is not a design error — Partner User Agreement §3.8. Every customer-facing surface that shows a design carries that notice.
