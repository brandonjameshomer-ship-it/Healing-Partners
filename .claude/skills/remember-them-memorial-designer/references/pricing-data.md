- Granite benches

- Fully custom shape work

## Lead times

| Item | Time |
|---|---|
| Granite, US-manufactured | **3–4 months** ✓ |
| Granite, India- or China-manufactured | **7–10 months** ✓ |
| Bronze elements | **~10 weeks**, tracked separately ✓ |

Bronze runs **in parallel** with granite, not after it. Order both at the same time or the bronze
becomes the critical path. State both timelines separately on the order form.

## Commission model

Charged to the funeral home. `$150/month` subscription applies at every tier; only the share of
marker sales changes.

| Memorials per month | Share |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | 15% |
| 6–19 | 12% |
| 20 or more | 10% |
