# Pricing data

**Source:** Affordable Family Memorials — *Wholesale Price Guide for 2025*, dated 1 Feb 2025.
Angie & Jason Pope · 6615 SE Harold St, Portland OR 97206 · 503-515-7640 · afmemorials@comcast.net

**All figures below are WHOLESALE.** Multiply by the markup to get the family price.

---

## The markup rule

> **Retail shown to a family = wholesale × 2.50**

Applies to granite, polish, inscription, artwork, installation, vases and porcelain.

> **The one exception: bench installation has a markup of 1.00 — no markup.** Pass it through at cost.

Never show a family a wholesale figure. Never apply 2.5 to bench installation.

## Commission

Charged to the funeral home. **Calculated on the retail price**, not wholesale. $150/month
subscription applies at every tier.

| Memorials per month | Share |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | 15% |
| 6–19 | 12% |
| 20 or more | 10% |
