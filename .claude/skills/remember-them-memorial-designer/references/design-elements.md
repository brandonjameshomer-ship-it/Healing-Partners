# Design elements

The catalogue of what can go on a memorial. Propose from here, **always tied to something the
family actually said**, and always checked against `design-constraints.md`.

Pricing: only the starred items have published wholesale prices. Everything else is **pending
supplier quote** — see `pricing-data.md`.

---

## Lettering and inscription

**Name and dates** — the irreducible minimum. Confirmed spelling, confirmed dates.

**Epitaph** — a line or two. Their own words are almost always better than a stock phrase. If the
family quotes something the person actually said, use that.

**Custom sandblasted quote line** ★ *(covered by the $25 custom art add-on)*

---

## Portraits

**Porcelain portrait, inset** ★ *($305 for 5×7 oval, colour)*
A physical tile mounted into the stone. Works on **any** granite colour. Choose this when the
family wants a photograph and is not set on black granite.

**Photo-realistic laser etching**
Requires **absolute black granite**. See `design-constraints.md`. Beautiful on the right stone,
unusable on the wrong one.

**Hand-drawn portrait etching** ★ *($25 custom art add-on)*
Line-art rather than photographic. More forgiving across stone colours.

---

## Sandblasted artwork ★ *($25 flat add-on)*

Cuts into the stone. Works on any colour or finish. The most versatile and least expensive way to
personalise, and the right default for most families.

Common treatments: vine and floral borders, a scene (ridge line, lake, farmstead), musical staves,
tools of a trade, vehicle silhouettes, book and reading glasses, animals.

## Glass *(pending supplier quote)*

Light passes through and changes across the day, which families often find moving. Confirm the
cemetery permits glass before proposing.

## Proposing well

Aim for six to nine suggestions so narrowing is a genuine choice. Each one needs a one-line
rationale naming the detail it came from.

Gate constrained items explicitly: *"A photo-etched portrait — but that only works if you go with
an absolute black stone, so it depends which way you lean on colour."*

Do not propose an element you cannot connect to something they told you. Generic filler is how a
family ends up with a stone that could belong to anyone.
