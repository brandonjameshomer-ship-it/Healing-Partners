About these charges

Every figure on this page is a term of the Remember Them Partner User Agreement rather than a product decision — the two subscription rates and the six-month initial term are Sec. 5.1, and the recapture is Sec. 5.2. If a figure here disagrees with your signed agreement, the agreement governs and we would like to know about it.

Commission on memorial sales is not paid here. It is reported and remitted by the fifteenth of the following month under Sec. 6.4.
